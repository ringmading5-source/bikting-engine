B-STAE knowledge and procedures

Store source knowledge and explicit procedures. Retrieve applicable procedures, compose bounded paths, and verify execution against the supplied target.

Source-backed knowledge

Source text is evidence; it does not automatically become an executable procedure.


Explicit procedure
Supported: add N; append "text"; brighten N; move by X,Y,Z; shift samples by N. Output checks verify the declared transformation, not real-world factual truth.


Stored composition
Definitions contain an explicit intent or registered child phrases. Missing and conflicting definitions stop execution.
